Problem Statement

Small and medium-sized businesses often struggle with manually resizing, optimising and categorising product images for their digital platforms.

This process is time-consuming, repetitive and difficult to scale. Large image files can also increase storage requirements and negatively affect website loading performance.

SmartThumb SA addresses this problem by providing an automated, serverless image-processing solution using AWS.** The system generates optimised thumbnails and uses AI-powered image recognition to identify and label uploaded images, reducing manual effort and improving operational efficiency.
